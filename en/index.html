Pension 48 · Špindlerův Mlýn

A quiet place in the heart of the Krkonoše Mountains.

A family-run guesthouse in a quiet part of Svatý Petr, surrounded by mountains within easy reach. Twenty beds across three types of accommodation, two shared kitchenettes and a peaceful garden — whether you're here to ski or to hike in summer.
