About the guesthouse

A cosy base in the Krkonoše foothills

Pension 48 sits in a quiet part of the Svatý Petr valley, just a short walk from both the slopes and the hiking trails. This family-run guesthouse offers 19 beds across its rooms and apartment, each with its own bathroom and views of Kozí hřbety, Stoh and Pláně.

Guests can use two shared kitchenettes, a ski boot dryer and a storage room for skis and bikes. Outside, there's a garden with seating and a playground with a trampoline and slide – making the guesthouse a natural choice for families with children and groups of friends alike.

The centre of Špindlerův Mlýn is about a 15-minute walk away, so you get peace and quiet while still having easy access to restaurants, shops and services.

Location

Svatý Petr & Špindlerův Mlýn

Pension 48 stands in a quiet part of the Svatý Petr valley – with the mountains right on the doorstep, yet only a short walk from the centre of it all.

Winter

Skiing on your doorstep

The Svatý Petr slope is about 400 m away, and the Stoh ski area is a 10-minute walk. There's also a snow tubing track about 100 m from the guesthouse.

Summer

Hiking in the Krkonoše Mountains

Svatý Petr is a great starting point for hiking and cycling trips around the Krkonoše Mountains National Park.

Špindlerův Mlýn

The centre, within walking distance

The centre of Špindlerův Mlýn, with its restaurants and shops, is about a 15-minute walk away. The guesthouse itself sits about 150 m from the main road, in a quiet part of the village.
