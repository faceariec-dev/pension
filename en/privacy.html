What data we process

When handling an inquiry or booking, we process in particular: your first and last name, email address, phone number, stay dates and length, number of guests, and any notes you provide via the inquiry form.

We process personal data in order to handle your inquiry, communicate about your booking, and provide accommodation services. We do not share your data with third parties except where required by law (e.g. guest registration under the Aliens Act, local tourist tax).

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, and to object to processing. If you have any questions, please contact us at pension48@centrum.cz.
